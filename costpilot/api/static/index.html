    What the engine refuses to do

    The engine will not allocate overhead with an unregistered driver, will not use an unapproved mapping, and will not compare products across different scopes. If a gate fails, the card says which gate blocked it and what to fix.

    Performance evaluation

    How to read the result

    Residual income is operating income less a capital charge on invested assets. A division can show a positive ROI and still destroy value if the charge exceeds income. The scorecard weights are fixed in the registry, not chosen by the model.
